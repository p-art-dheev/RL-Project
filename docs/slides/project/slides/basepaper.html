Base paper

MERSEM (Ramicetty et al., 2026): evolutionary search guided by Q-learning

What it does

  • Edge–fog–cloud offloading of graph-analytics DAG jobs
  • Objective: w_s · SLA-violation rate + w_c · gCO₂ (fixed weights)
  • Every 15-min epoch: a population of job→VM mappings, crossover + mutation
  • A Q-table picks how many jobs to perturb (2–15%) during local search

What it claims

−45%

SLA violations vs metaheuristics

−12%

carbon vs metaheuristics

Evaluated in extended EdgeCloudSim only; baselines are GWO, CSA, SSA and PSO-GA.

Also read: DeepRM (Mao et al., HotNets 2016) and DeepRM2 (Ye et al., 2018). We borrow their online RL scheduling and behaviour-cloning warm start.

MERSEM is the only one of the three given papers that targets edge–cloud with SLA and carbon, so it is the paper we must beat. DeepRM and DeepRM2 only schedule one pooled cluster with no network, so beating them would not say much here.
