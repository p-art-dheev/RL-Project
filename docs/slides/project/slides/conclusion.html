Takeaways

What we built and what it shows

1

Online, task-level RL beats the 2026 base paper on its own metric: fewer SLA violations and less carbon, with p = 0.002.

2

With a w-dependent shield it matches a tuned heuristic on analytics deadlines while cutting carbon by about a quarter, and it degrades least under rush hours and failures.

3

The same code schedules real machines in Mumbai and Stockholm from a laptop in Coimbatore, which you are about to see live.

Code, data and runbook: github.com/p-art-dheev/RL-Project

Close by inviting questions. Likely ones: why RL and not a heuristic (state-dependent trade-offs, a runtime knob, robustness; and we show where the heuristic still wins); is comparing online vs offline fair (oracle MERSEM, generous fallback, job-level PACE control); how is carbon measured on AWS (CPU time × per-vCPU power × PUE × location-based grid intensity).
