On the first four rows PACE beats both baselines in 10 of 10 seeds (p = 0.002) with less carbon. On the slow WAN it keeps sending alerts to Stockholm: the same weakness the real testbed showed when the network drifted.

E6, simulation, 10 paired seeds, PACE w = 0.9, synthetic carbon. Training covered 2–6 edge, 1–3 fog, 1–3 cloud nodes, Mumbai + Stockholm, RTT ×0.7–1.5. Details: results/E6_GENERALISATION.md

This is the zero-shot test: same weights, no fine-tuning. The size-agnostic node scorer is why the larger city works; we only had to let the candidate buffer grow past the 12 nodes used in training. The honest failure is the slow WAN: network conditions far outside training confuse the policy, while CA-EFT simply recomputes finish times. The fix is the same as on the real testbed: an online network estimate plus wider network randomisation in training.
