Carbon −26% (p = 0.002); analytics 6.0% vs 5.6% is within noise (± 6 pp CI). Without the shield, PACE still sends too many large analytics jobs north. CA-EFT alone reaches 3% analytics misses, at higher carbon.

Shield = drop nodes predicted to miss the task's sub-deadline; strictness follows w (Alshiekh et al., AAAI 2018). The learned policy still picks among the remaining nodes.

This is the honest slide. Pure PACE wins on alerts and carbon but loses on large analytics DAGs; held-out jobs are bigger (median 25 tasks vs 15 in training). We diagnosed it: those jobs need 200+ CPU-seconds and the green region has 4 cores. The shield plus job-pressure features fix most of it. Remaining gap at very low miss rates is future work (a GNN job encoder, training on larger DAGs).
