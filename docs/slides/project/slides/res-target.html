A loose 5% target gives 2.3% violations and spends the headroom on carbon (−25% vs w = 0.9). A fixed-weight method like MERSEM can only reach a target by re-tuning and re-running its search.

Mechanism: w is an input of the policy, so changing w changes behaviour instantly. A PI controller raises w when recent alert and analytics misses exceed the target and lowers it when there is headroom; this is the deployment-time analogue of a Lagrange multiplier. In the live demo we move the target from 5% to 1% on the dashboard.
