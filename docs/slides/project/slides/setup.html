Experimental setup

Same data, same traces, same seeds for every method

ItemChoice
WorkloadMERSEM's own DAG dataset (Zenodo 4667690): 12,000 jobs sampled; train on the first 70%, test on the held-out last 30%
Job classesA camera alerts (1 s, 30% of feeds privacy-restricted), B analytics (tight), C batch (30–120 min, deferrable)
TopologiesTestbed mirror (2 edge, 2 Mumbai, 2 Stockholm nodes) and a larger city (8 edge, 3 Mumbai, 4 Stockholm)
BaselinesMERSEM (re-implemented, + an oracle variant), CA-EFT (tuned, 5 margins), EFT, Job-EFT, carbon-greedy, edge/fog/cloud-only, random
MetricsSLA-violation rate (all jobs and per class), dynamic gCO₂, p95 alert latency, hypervolume, decision time
Statistics10 paired seeds (each a different day, hour, workload and carbon trace), mean ± 95% CI, Wilcoxon signed-rank
Fairness choices we made in MERSEM's favour: it gets oracle knowledge of the next epoch's arrivals, its population is seeded with a greedy mapping, and if its planned node fails we fall back to EFT immediately. We also tuned CA-EFT's safety margin so the strongest simple heuristic is really strong.
